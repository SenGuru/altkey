How it works

Three commands. Two subscriptions you already pay for.

altkey runs on your machine and speaks OpenAI's API on the front, your AI subscription's OAuth on the back. Nothing leaves your laptop except the model calls themselves.

What it doesn't do

Your subscriptions stay yours.

altkey is software. Not a service. It runs on your laptop, reads the credential files your CLIs already wrote, and never sends them anywhere.

Never proxies your tokens.

OAuth lives in ~/.codex/auth.json and ~/.claude/.credentials.json, where they already were. altkey reads them in-process. They don't leave your machine.

Never reads your history.

altkey speaks the model APIs. Your provider's chat history, settings, and account data live in the provider's web UI. altkey can't see them. Doesn't want to.

Never phones home.

The free CLI is bound to 127.0.0.1. Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. Zero outbound traffic that isn't a model call you asked for.

Open source.

Single Rust binary. Read the code. Build it yourself. Audit the OAuth refresh code in providers/ in under an hour.

Questions you should be asking

Asked, answered honestly.

Is this against ChatGPT's or Anthropic's terms of service?

Personal use of your own subscription through an OAuth flow is the same posture as the official Codex CLI and Claude Code — both of which hit the exact endpoints altkey hits. We don't pool subscriptions, we don't relay your sub to anyone else, and we never proxy your tokens. As long as it's you, on your machine, for your work, you're inside both the spirit and the letter of the agreements.

Can my account get banned for using this?

Highly unlikely for personal-volume use. If you proxy altkey out to the public internet and serve other people's requests with your sub, yes that can get your account banned (and you'd be re-selling). Don't do that. Use it for your own work on your own machine and you're fine.

Does it work with Claude Code itself?

Yes. Point ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL at altkey and Claude Code keeps working — the native /v1/messages endpoint is a full passthrough with the attestation header injected.

What about Gemini, Llama, Mistral?

Not yet, by choice. Google has no free OAuth image-gen path (Pro covers it only inside the gemini.google.com web app; CLI/API/Vertex all bill per image). We'll add Gemini when we can do it without rebuilding the cookie-scraping machinery we deliberately killed. Llama / Mistral show up when there's a clean personal-hosting story.

What's the difference between Free, Cloud, and Pro?

Free is the binary — runs on your laptop, hits your subs, end of story. Cloud adds the multi-machine + dashboard pieces that need a hosted account. Pro adds the things we ship as a product on top of altkey itself: pre-patched adapters for tools that don't respect OPENAI_BASE_URL, team workspaces, and priority requests.

Where does the name come from?

"alt-key" — the alternative key to the door you've already paid the rent for. Also alt + key as in option + key on your keyboard. We liked both.
