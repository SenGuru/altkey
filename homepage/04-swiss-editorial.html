/ 06 FAQ

Honest answers.

The questions developers actually ask before they connect an account.

Q1 How is it billed?

Two separate things. You pay altkey a flat monthly fee — $5 Starter or $25 Pro — for the proxy, uptime, and dashboard. The actual model usage is drawn from the ChatGPT Plus or Claude subscription you already pay the provider for. There are no per-token API charges from altkey.

Q2 Which providers are supported?

Today: OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude). Connect one or both — a single altkey key reaches them both, and you choose the provider per request via the model field. More providers are coming soon.

Q3 Is my login safe? Do you store my password?

OAuth only. You log in through the provider's official authorization screen, and altkey receives a scoped, revocable token — never your password. We don't store credentials and we don't scrape your account.

Q4 Can I revoke access?

Anytime. Disconnect a provider from your altkey dashboard, or revoke the token directly in your OpenAI or Anthropic account settings. Access stops immediately — your subscriptions stay yours.

Q5 How is this different from the official API?

The official OpenAI and Anthropic APIs bill you per token, separately from your chat subscription. altkey lets you make programmatic calls that run on the $20/mo subscription you already have — so you're not paying twice. It's a convenience proxy for personal use, subject to each provider's own limits and terms.

Q6 Is there a free trial?

There's no free tier yet — Starter begins at $5/month, which is intentionally low so you can try altkey against a real workload without committing much. You can cancel anytime and revoke connected accounts on the way out.
