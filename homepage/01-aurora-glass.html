How it works

From login to live key in three steps.

No API console, no billing setup, no new credit card. Connect what you already own and start calling.

Drop-in compatible

Change two lines. Keep your whole stack.

altkey speaks the OpenAI API, so the OpenAI SDK, LangChain, Cursor, your own scripts — anything that takes a base_url and an api_key — just works.

  • Same request and response shapes you already use.
  • One endpoint reaches both OpenAI and Anthropic models.

app.py 
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    base_url="https://api.altkey.dev/v1",
    api_key="ak-live-7Qx2...c4F9",
)

stream = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="claude-3-5-sonnet",
    messages=[{"role": "user",
               "content": "Refactor this for me."}],
    stream=True,
)
for chunk in stream:
    print(chunk.choices[0].delta.content or "", end="")
request.sh 
curl https://api.altkey.dev/v1/chat/completions \

The honest details on billing, providers, and security.

Two parts. You pay altkey a flat monthly fee — $5 on Starter or $25 on Pro — for the proxy and infrastructure. The actual model usage runs through your connected ChatGPT Plus or Claude account, so it counts against the subscription you already pay for, not a separate per-token API bill.
Today altkey supports OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude). You can connect one or both and reach their models through a single key and endpoint. More providers are coming soon.
You sign in on OpenAI’s and Anthropic’s own pages — altkey never sees or stores your password. We receive a scoped, revocable OAuth token that lets us route your requests, and nothing more. No scraping, no credential storage.
Anytime. Disconnect a provider from your altkey dashboard, or revoke the token directly from your OpenAI or Anthropic account settings. The moment you do, altkey can no longer route requests through that account.
The official OpenAI and Anthropic APIs bill you separately, per token, on top of any chat subscription you hold. altkey instead routes programmatic calls through the subscription you already pay for — so you get API-style access at your existing flat subscription cost, not per-token API rates on top of it.
altkey starts at just $5/mo on the Starter plan, so there’s no big commitment to test it on your real workflow. You can cancel and revoke access at any time — no long-term contract.
