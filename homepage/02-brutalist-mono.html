[06] FAQ

Straight answers.

06
Q1How is it billed?+
Two things. You pay altkey a flat monthly fee — $5 on Starter or $25 on Pro — for the proxy and key. The actual model requests run against the AI subscription you already pay for (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro), so there's no separate per-token API charge stacked on top.
Q2Which providers are supported?+
Today: OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude). You can connect one or both and reach them through a single key and endpoint. More providers are coming soon.
Q3Is my login safe? Do you store my password?+
We never see or store your password. You authorize altkey through each provider's official OAuth flow, which hands us a scoped token — not your credentials. We don't scrape and we don't run headless browsers against your account.
Q4Can I revoke access?+
Yes, anytime — from your altkey dashboard or directly in your provider's connected-apps settings. Once disconnected, your key immediately stops routing to that account. Your subscriptions stay fully yours.
Q5What's the difference vs. the official API?+
The official APIs bill you per token, separately from any consumer plan you hold. altkey routes requests through the consumer subscription you already pay for, so you get programmatic access without a second metered bill. In return you work within that subscription's usage, and altkey adds a thin proxy layer for the key and endpoint.
Q6Is there a free trial?+
There's no free tier today — Starter is a low $5/mo so the barrier stays small. You can cancel anytime, and disconnecting your accounts is always one click away.
