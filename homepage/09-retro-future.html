app.py 
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    base_url="https://api.altkey.dev/v1",
    api_key="ak-live-7Qx2...Yh9",
)

stream = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="claude-3-5-sonnet",
    messages=[{"role": "user",
               "content": "Refactor this for me"}],
    stream=True,
)
for chunk in stream:
    print(chunk.choices[0].delta.content or "", end="")

Questions

Honest answers

How is this different from buying an API key from OpenAI or Anthropic?
Official API keys bill you per token, on top of any subscription you already have. altkey routes requests through your existing ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro plan instead, so programmatic usage draws from the subscription you're already paying for — you just pay altkey a small flat fee for the proxy.
Which providers are supported?
Today, OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude). You can connect one or both under a single key. More providers are coming soon.
Do I have to give you my password?
No. altkey uses OAuth only. You log in through the provider's official flow and we receive a revocable token — never your password. There's no credential storage and no scraping. You can revoke access at any time.
Is it really OpenAI-compatible?
Yes. Point any OpenAI SDK (or plain curl) at https://api.altkey.dev/v1 with your altkey key. The chat completions schema, streaming, and tool calls work as a drop-in replacement — change the base URL and key, leave the rest of your code alone.
What are the rate limits?
Your throughput is bounded by the subscription you connect — altkey doesn't grant extra capacity beyond your plan. Starter is sized for individual use on the $20 Plus/Pro plans; the $25 Pro tier raises altkey's own limits and adds priority routing for heavier workloads.
Is altkey reliable enough for production?
altkey runs on dedicated VPS infrastructure with 24/7 uptime — no cold starts or free-tier throttling. That said, your effective availability also depends on the upstream provider and your own subscription staying in good standing.
